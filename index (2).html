Our Story

Honouring Tradition,
Inspiring Innovation

Tucked into the vibrant energy of Great Portland Street, Faros Oxford Circus is a place where Mediterranean warmth meets Italian craftsmanship. We opened our doors with one simple idea — that great food should feel like a gathering, not just a meal.

Every dish we serve carries the soul of coastal Mediterranean kitchens. Sun-ripened tomatoes, hand-rolled pasta, the kind of olive oil that makes you pause. We source seasonal ingredients, work with trusted suppliers, and cook in a way that respects where everything comes from.

This isn't fine dining behind glass. It's the sort of place where the table feels like yours, the staff know their regulars by name, and the food keeps you coming back — week after week, season after season.

What We Offer

More Than Just a Meal

🍽️

Dine In

A relaxed, welcoming dining room right in the heart of Oxford Circus. Perfect for date nights, family lunches, or a solo escape with a glass of Barolo and a good pasta.

🎉

Private Events

Planning something special? We host birthday dinners, corporate lunches, and celebration evenings. Speak to us about semi-private and full buyout options.

📦

Takeaway

Craving Faros at home? Our takeaway menu brings the same kitchen quality — packed with care and ready when you are. Call ahead or walk in.

🥂

Aperitivo & Drinks

Not ready to sit down just yet? Pull up to the bar. Our Aperitivo selection — spritz, negroni, chilled whites — is made to ease you into the evening properly.

🧑‍🍳

Chef's Tasting Menu

For something a little more considered, ask about our seasonal tasting menu. Available Friday & Saturday evenings, with optional wine pairing.

Close deals over good food. We're five minutes from Oxford Circus tube, with reliable, unhurried service and a menu that impresses without being fussy.
